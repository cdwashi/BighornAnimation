## Scope — one derivation, one allocation rule, one round

   - grid 10 m; shelf membership `|elevation − e0| ≤ 3.5` m where `e0` = elevation at the bench point (scenario `coverFeatures` entry `bench` via `terrain.toLocal`);
   - edge cell = shelf cell with any of 8 neighbors at 30 m outward reach whose elevation sits > 3.5 m below `e0`;
   - segment window: bearings 45°–135° from the bench point, within 200 m.
   The extraction on current terrain must reproduce the forty-fourth measurement's committed numbers (103 cells, north–south span 260 m, no gap > 20 m along N–S) — pinned in a named test. Order the cell list by its north–south coordinate so partition is stable.
2. **Goal allocation** (`engine/src/camp-defense.ts`): when a band's camp-defence selection lands on `scenario-bench`, its goal is not the feature point but its lip slot: let `assigned` = the bands currently holding `campDefense.featureId === 'scenario-bench'` **plus the band being assigned**, ordered by unit id; partition the ordered cell list into `|assigned|` equal contiguous segments; the band's goal is the middle cell of the segment at its index. When the assigned set changes (join, release, destruction), holders' goals refresh to their new slots through the existing held-goal/re-path machinery on its existing cadence — no new timing rule. A band alone on the feature takes the middle of the whole arc.
3. **Fallback, defensive only:** if extraction returns an empty cell list (cannot happen on current terrain — the pinned test proves it), fall back to the feature point and flag `TODO-AMBIGUOUS`; do not invent a substitute geometry.
