## Context

The model has no ammunition-independent killing channel: fire requires rounds (the coalition is dry by ~minute 780 with no resupply), D65 shock resolution writes morale and engagement state only, and the removed-by-this-WO cohesion-floor conversion dissolved escaped units on a timer. Measured consequences (preserved probes, D105 evidence column): pursuers hold contact at 50–185 m for thirty minutes and never convert; a reachable melee without lethality made every casualty figure worse; the historical record (O8, `docs/research/Close Action Melee Research.md`) describes close action as the resolution of a closing event — "a brief firefight followed by the close-range dispatch of the wounded" — and refuses a rate.

**The re-armed stop is live this round** (`docs/PREDICTIONS.md`): halt for adjudication if Reno A/G/M killed exceeds 60 in more than 5/50 registered seeds, or any seed reaches killed ≥ 100. Never tune in response to results, in either direction.

## Scope — three coupled changes plus one removal, one round

1. **The pursuer may arrive** (`engine/src/movement.ts`): the COMBAT-pursuit movement standoff becomes `meleeRangeMeters` (25) instead of `pursuitCloseRangeMeters` (50), at both standoff sites. All other standoff semantics (INITIATIVE pursuit, CHARGE-at-zero, default 150 m) unchanged.
2. **Shock accepts the pursuer** (`engine/src/combat.ts`, `resolveShock`): when no CHARGE-postured attacker exists in a MELEE-state engagement, a unit whose COMBAT pursuit targets the other party is the attacker. The D65 shock formula, both margins, and all multipliers are byte-unchanged.
3. **The bout — event-latched resolution with emergent outcomes:**
   - One shock resolution per pursuer-quarry pair per contact episode. **Re-eligibility on contact-break only**: the pair's engagement leaving MELEE state (range rising above `meleeRangeMeters`) re-arms the latch; there is no timer, no cadence, no per-interval re-check. Implement the latch on the engagement descriptor so it serializes with state.
   - **Break outcome** (shock > defense × `chargeBreakMargin`): the defender is set ROUTED exactly as today, and **the D81 exception fires: the defender's wounded convert to killed** — `killed += wounded; wounded = 0`; `strengthCurrent`/`strengthAvailable` untouched (wounded were already outside current strength per D81's accounting); `casualties` unchanged (already counted). This is the ONLY place in the engine where wounded convert. Flight is the broken fragment's default via existing rout machinery.
   - **Repel outcome** (shock < defense × `chargeRepelMargin`): the attacker's COMBAT pursuit ENDS via the existing pursuit-termination path (reason string may say `repulsed`). Not a stand-off, not a pause — termination. The existing repel posture write (`WITHDRAW`) stands.
   - **Middle**: no conversion, no new behavior; existing engagement semantics continue.
   - **Emit a `melee-bout` event** on every resolution: `{tick, unitId (attacker), targetUnitId (defender), outcome: 'break'|'repel'|'held', convertedWounded}`. This event is load-bearing: PR-32's and PR-36's audit legs are graded from it.
4. **The drain interlock — removal** (`engine/src/morale.ts`): the `unit.cohesion <= config.destructionCohesionFloor` clause is REMOVED from the destruction test. Destruction at `strengthCurrent <= destructionStrengthFloor` remains (and converts nothing at zero strength). The morale/cohesion pressure drain itself (`routCohesionDrain` while ROUTED) is UNCHANGED — it produces the break, it no longer performs the killing.

**Tests, named:** `bout-latch-one-resolution-per-contact` (a pair in continuous melee resolves once; separation and re-entry re-arms), `repulse-ends-pursuit` (repelled pursuer's pursuit is terminated, not paused), `break-converts-wounded-in-bout-only` (conversion fires on break, and nowhere else — flight, repulse, fire, and rout produce zero conversions), `no-cohesion-floor-destruction` (a routed unit at cohesion floor with positive strength is NOT destroyed), `standoff-closes-to-melee` (a COMBAT pursuer's movement target reaches melee range of its quarry).
