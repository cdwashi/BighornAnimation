## Hypotheses, registered — each stated with its account of the flagship

- **H-TRACK (live target tracking):** these terminals are OTHER UNITS' POSITIONS. Some
  behaviour paths units at entities rather than places — pursuit, escort, engagement —
  and the recorded terminal is the target's position at path-computation time. The
  ladders are a moving target's track sampled at recomputation intervals (spacing =
  target speed × interval); the pairing is two units pathing at the same target, or one
  unit's target pair; the cross-side sharing is coupled geometry — pursuer terminals ride
  the pursued unit's track. **Flagship account:** routed units flee to the clamp point
  and HALT there; every unit pathing at them thereafter computes the same terminal, on
  both sides, in any morale state — 493 events collapse onto the halt point. **Registered
  constraint binding the two accounts (Amendment 1): motion-ladders and stasis-stacks
  must be ONE mechanism on ONE population — the rung targets and the flagship stack are
  the same units at different phases, rungs while the target moves, stack once it halts.
  The registered check: ladder rungs on a reference line terminate at that line's clamp
  point, with the same targets. If the rung population and the flagship population are
  disjoint units, H-TRACK is two hypotheses under one name and fails the
  flagship-coverage constraint.**
- **H-HORIZON (truncated planning window):** the pathfinder plans a bounded distance
  ahead, and the recorded terminal is the sliding window's end, not the journey's. The
  ladders are successive horizon ends along one long route (spacing = replan advance);
  the pairing is two units marching the same route in step; the sharing is both sides
  routing along the same line toward the same far point. **Flagship account:** the final
  window of every long north-bound route on x=6624 ends at the clamped route end, where
  all windows converge. **Registered burden, from a seen constraint:** order-driven
  marches in the D5 traces show NO intermediate rungs — H-HORIZON must hold that ladders
  appear on ordered long marches at scale, or explain at registration why only un-ordered
  movement windows; it takes the burden as stated and predicts LADDERS ON ORDERED MARCHES
  at scale.
- **H-STEP (quantised flight computation):** flight destinations are computed from the
  MOVER'S OWN position as a fixed or quantised displacement along an axis, clamped at
  bounds. The ladders are different origins or different step counts along the same axis
  line; the pairing is two units fleeing abreast; the sharing is its WEAKEST claim,
  stated: cross-side arrival at identical exact points must be coincidence of computed
  values, which 473-of-606 makes expensive. **Flagship account:** long flights from
  anywhere south on the x=6624 line clamp at maxY and stack there.

## Discriminators, with each hypothesis's expectation stated in advance

All are semi-seen (the committed Probe R1 log and the audited data surface; no
simulation) unless marked; the ruling weighs them per the standing class.

- **S1 — rung ownership.** Who owns the ladder-rung events: the units moving along the
  line, or units elsewhere pathing AT something on the line? Tabulate per rung: owning
  unit, side, morale, position at event (from the trace context), enemy distance.
  H-TRACK: rungs owned by units whose own position is AWAY from the rung (they path at
  it), with small distance to a specific enemy whose track passes the rung; H-HORIZON and
  H-STEP: rungs owned by the units ON the line (terminal ahead of own position along it).
- **S2 — step arithmetic against declared speeds.** Rung spacings (254–378 m) against the
  movement speeds the data surface declares, wherever it declares them, times candidate
  intervals. H-TRACK: spacing ≈ TARGET's declared speed × a consistent interval;
  H-HORIZON: spacing ≈ a constant advance independent of any unit's speed; H-STEP:
  spacing ≈ the OWNER's speed-independent fixed quantum. The spacings visibly vary
  (254–378, seen) — a hypothesis needing strict constancy must explain the variance at
  registration or accept the burden.
- **S3 — temporal coupling at shared points.** At the flagship, tick-interleaving of
  cross-side events: H-TRACK predicts pursuer events FOLLOW pursued arrivals and cluster
  behind them in time, with pursuit-range enemy distances; H-HORIZON and H-STEP predict
  no temporal ordering between sides beyond battle tempo.
- **S4 — the ordered-march control.** Order-driven terminal changes at scale: do declared
  long marches ever show intermediate rungs? H-HORIZON: YES, necessarily (its kill-shot
  if no); H-TRACK and H-STEP: no — ordered objectives are places, not entities, and
  flights are one computation.
- **S5 — pairing identity.** The ×2 rungs: same two units across all sixteen? H-TRACK:
  the pair is a pursuer set locked onto one target group; H-HORIZON/H-STEP: the pair is
  the marching/fleeing pair itself. Names, from the log.

**Separability:** S1 splits TRACK from the other two on ownership; S4 splits HORIZON from
both; S2 splits STEP's constant quantum from TRACK's target-speed product; S3 corroborates
TRACK independently; S5 cross-checks whichever survives. No two hypotheses predict the
