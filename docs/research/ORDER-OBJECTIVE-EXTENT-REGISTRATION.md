## §1 — The ledger search (third execution; run first; complete)

- **D108 — the direct precedent, and its constraint is BINDING, not advisory:** Bench
  extent as goal geometry — the lip, not the area; allocation at band granularity; **no
  per-man figure, no new [CAL]: where the literature returns no number, the mechanism

  radius in metres must say where the number comes from; "it looks about right" is the
  thing D108 refused. The lip was an arc DERIVED FROM TERRAIN, not a chosen distance.
- **D88/D90 — the compounding fact, registered as an independent argument for extent:**
  feature NAMES are the load-bearing locators; lat/lon carry high positional
  uncertainty. A dimensionless objective is a precise point standing for an imprecise
  place — an argument that exists regardless of the stacking, registered here rather
  than discovered later.
- **D91 — the midpoint retirement:** camp-defence goals moved from geometric point to
  feature-based positioning. Whether the same move is available for order objectives
  depends on whether the objective HAS a feature: landmark-form objectives do;
  waypoint-form objectives are points by historical declaration; entity-form objectives
  (targetUnitId) have none. **The asymmetry is a design-space constraint.**
- **D53/D45 — order-objective coherence** is an existing principle (landmarks placed for
  it); **METHODS §7 — the D91 rider**, whose adjudication this item is.
- **The `marchSpacingMeters` asymmetry, registered as a question of its own (added at

  and applies it at one end of a march but not the other. Deliberate-but-undocumented,

  THE EXISTING MECHANISM APPLIED AT THE DESTINATION, with `marchSpacingMeters` already
  ruled as a [CAL]: a much easier design argument and a much narrower exposure.
