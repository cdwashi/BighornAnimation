relative to that tick's combat resolution and morale update — and is the finishing
predicate's evaluation point the actual mechanism behind the timing fact?**

The timing fact, from M2: the shelterer population at end of t−1 contains **99 STEADY

that breaks its companions. That is not a fact about WHO counts (eligibility); it is a
fact about WHEN the counting happens. D113's snapshot-versus-vector phrase, carried as
prose since it was written, now has a number attached to a mechanism nobody has
specified: every relation in the five-relation inventory evaluates at some point inside
the tick, and no registration anywhere states where.
