## AMENDMENT 1 (2026-08-02, adjudicated after the first-patch STOP; original text above untouched)

**The gap, with authorship:** the one-break clause above reads "The hash will change — once. A
second change is a STOP." That rule counts HASH CHANGES. The thing it protects is ATTRIBUTION,
and attribution is damaged only when a measurement is taken against an intermediate stream.
The two coincide in every case anyone had in mind and diverge in the one Codex hit — a byte
corrected at the editor before anything observed it. Correlate-versus-cause, the session's
sixth instance, the adjudicator's authorship, frozen into a work order that passed four review
rounds. Codex's `TODO-AMBIGUOUS(D111-STOP)` named the gap exactly and took the stricter
reading against its own interest.

**The rule, re-anchored to observation:** a stream is ACCEPTED at the first ENGINE EXECUTION
against its bytes — oracle refresh, pin run, campaign tick, registered computation, **or any
informal run whatsoever**. Before acceptance, editor-time corrections are permitted; after it,
any edit is a second break and a STOP. The rationale is written in, not just the rule: you
cannot fit to a stream nobody has observed — editor-time iteration carries zero information
about outcomes and so cannot bias anything; the moment a run carries information, the
permission ends.

**Disclosure:** every discarded intermediate hash is recorded in the report by name, with a
count of editor-time states. `9994ffa6` is the first: transient, never observed, refused as a
baseline. The PRIMARY protection remains the semantic-diff gate — the accepted payload equals
the frozen five edits against HEAD exactly — and the disclosure is what makes the permission
auditable rather than unbounded.

**The three gating ambiguities, ruled:**

1. **Closing predicate (D111-APPROACH):** F is the nearest eligible friendly at the
   annihilation tick under D107's own predicate (same-side combat unit, STEADY, not destroyed,
   not withdrawn), positions at that tick, held fixed. The column takes THREE values:
   `closing` (distance at the annihilation tick < distance at the unit's last movement tick),
   `opening` (greater), `stationary` (no movement tick to compare — a unit annihilated in
   place is a different case from one annihilated opening, and collapsing them loses the
   distinction the instrument exists to expose). The predicate is adopted because it is
   CONSTRUCTIBLE from D107's predicate and the unit's own motion with nothing invented; that
   it reproduces the D108 breach row (co-m, 865 m, closing) is a CHECK on the instrument, not
   its justification — had it failed to, that would have signalled the predicate was wrong.
   Note for the record: D107's isolation test is a static radius snapshot, and the named bench
   item is snapshot-versus-vector; this table records both halves per annihilation — the first
   measurement designed to expose that gap rather than discover it by accident.
2. **Table columns (D111-POSITION-SIDE):** position (x, y), channel side (WEST/EAST), and
   belligerent side, all recorded.
3. **Scope (D111-ANNIHILATIONS):** every belligerent unit, unqualified — a distribution
   instrument; scoping to Reno would presuppose the question it exists to answer.

**The four mechanical items:** (a) gate coverage at WO stage is the hook's staged-check
semantics; the suite gate reads HEAD/index blobs and binds at commit — no staging games,
staging stays untouched. (b) Re-baseline artifacts: `reports/d111-ground-pressure-census.*`
and `reports/d111-valley-range.*`, schema mirroring the `.claude/` precedents. (c) Hash-pin
scope: current stream-specific pins in tests/oracles only; immutable historical records
(D107/D108 result files, ledger rows, past reports) are never touched. (d) The annihilation
table spans every belligerent unit, per item 3.
