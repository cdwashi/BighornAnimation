1. **Already routed:** the defender's `moraleState` was `ROUTED` on entry to the resolution (read before the branch writes — first-break defenders are never annihilation-eligible).
2. **Isolated:** no eligible friendly within `isolationRadiusMeters` (650) of the defender, where **eligible = same-side combat unit (not `NONCOMBATANT_CAMP`), `moraleState === 'STEADY'`, not `endState`, and NOT `withdrawnOffField`** — the last clause is part of the ruled predicate (D107 row cites its origin: an instrument that omitted it counted withdrawn scouts' frozen positions as steady friendlies).

**Event extension (load-bearing for PR-47's audit legs):** `melee-bout` gains outcome value `'annihilation'` with `convertedWounded` keeping its meaning and an additive `terminalConverted` field (the strength converted at destruction). Additionally, a break-outcome bout whose defender was already routed but NOT isolated emits its event with an additive `shelteredBy` field: the nearest eligible friendly's `{id, distanceMeters, strengthCurrent}` — the which-one-specifically practice instrumented in the mechanism, and the reopen signal for the formed question (a shelterer materially below the 38th's observed floor — strength 29, ratio 73% — reopens it).
